psychological  distress  and  cardiometabolic  health  risks  during  and  after  treatment,
impacting their overall quality of life. Women with BC, particularly those receiving certain
treatments  (left-sided  radiation  therapy and endocrine therapy), may have a higher risk
of  developing hypertension, dyslipidemia, and diabetes.This study aims to evaluate the
feasibility  and  effects  of  a  novel  12-week  intervention  combining  physical  activity  and
virtual  reality (VR)-based mindfulness training in patients undergoing chemotherapy for
early-stage breast cancer.

This  study  will  recruit  10  participants  from  the  Stony  Brook  Cancer  Center,  assessing
feasibility  through  recruitment,  retention,  adherence,  and  satisfaction.  The intervention
will  include  a  patient-centered,  home-based  aerobic  and  resistance  exercise program,
alongside  immersive  VR  mindfulness  delivered  three  times  weekly.  Outcomes  will
include  changes  in  perceived  stress,  cardiometabolic  indicators  (e.g.,  blood  pressure,
body  composition,  exercise  endurance),  and  biomarkers  (e.g.,  cortisol  ,C-reactive
protein, and lipids).

Through  an  interdisciplinary  and  patient-centered  approach,  this  project  seeks  to
improve  mental  and  physical  health  outcomes  through  exercise  and  immersive  VR
mindfulness. If successful, these results could inform scalable interventions that support
survivorship and reduce associated comorbidities in breast cancer care.

Project Summary

Older adults face increased challenges with driving and community mobility due to natural aging or
conditions. Despite these needs, many do not receive appropriate rehabilitative services. This proposed
study builds on a pilot project led by the PI team to develop a mobile health application designed to
enhance driver assessment.

The current study aims to advance the app’s capabilities to evaluate real-world driving behaviors
alongside standardized clinical assessments. Older drivers and their passenger caregivers will use the app
to log real-time performance and safety observations, extending the scope of traditional clinic-based
evaluations. This will generate objective insights from naturalistic driving experiences to inform drivers,
caregivers, and providers about safety and functional needs.

The PI, an expert in mobile health and technology, will lead development to improve accessibility and
usability across care settings. The Co-PI, a certified Driving Rehabilitation Professional and NYS
Certified Driving Instructor, will oversee clinical assessments and translate results into practical,
real-world recommendations. Collaborating with the Department Chair of Neurology, this
interdisciplinary effort will establish a foundation for expanding driver rehabilitation services in Long
Island.

This project aligns with SHP Seed Grant priorities: healthcare innovation, interdisciplinary collaboration,
and technology integration for underserved populations.

**A. Personal Statement**As a licensed occupational therapist and Certified Hand Therapist with over 20 years of clinical experience across skilled nursing facilities, outpatient rehab, and acute care, I have dedicated my career to improving function and safety for older adults. My work has consistently focused on maximizing independence, reducing fall risk, and addressing age-related challenges through evidence-based assessment and intervention.

During my tenure as Regional Director of Clinical Education for 30+ skilled nursing facilities across three states, I led fall prevention initiatives that included staff training, root cause analysis, and interdisciplinary care planning to reduce fall rates. I developed and implemented individualized treatment plans that emphasized strength, balance, coordination, environmental safety, and activity modification—key components of effective fall prevention.

Now, as a Clinical Assistant Professor at Stony Brook University, I am committed to applying these clinical skills in an academic setting through the development of an interdisciplinary fall prevention program with OT and PT students. I have mentored student capstone projects and participated in community outreach efforts, including home safety assessments and implementation of fall prevention education for underserved older adults on Long Island. My clinical perspective ensures that students are not only grounded in theory but also equipped with the hands-on skills to assess fall risk, adapt environments, and empower older adults to remain active and independent.

Through this grant, I will leverage my extensive clinical background to ensure the fall prevention program is both impactful and sustainable, combining rigorous clinical reasoning with collaborative, community-focused care.

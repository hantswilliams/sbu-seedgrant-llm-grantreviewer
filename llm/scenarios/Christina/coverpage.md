**Title of Research Project**

Advancing Community Health and Clinical Education: A Study of Fall Risk Reduction and Interprofessional Collaboration Among OT and PT Students

**Total amount of budget requested:** $4995.00

**Project summary**

The Stony Brook University Interprofessional Falls Prevention Program aims to reduce fall risk among older adults on Long Island, focusing on underserved communities in the East End. This student-led, faculty-supervised initiative provides vital experiential learning for OT and PT students while addressing a critical service gap.

Suffolk County has the highest fall-related hospitalization rate in New York, and the East End—where up to 32% of the population are seniors—faces limited access to evidence-based, in-person fall prevention services. In 2023, falls accounted for over half of trauma admissions at Stony Brook Southampton Hospital, yet fewer than half of these older adults received in-person interventions.

To help close this gap, the proposed 8-week program will be offered weekly on the Southampton campus. It will combine education and intervention for older adults while aligning with Stony Brook’s mission of improving lives through healthcare education and community engagement.

This initiative will deliver essential services to a vulnerable population while fostering interdisciplinary collaboration and clinical skill development among future health professionals. The program aims to serve as a sustainable model for community-based falls prevention, improving long-term health outcomes for local seniors.

# School of Health Professions Research Seed Grant Review Instructions (Human Reviewers)

## Purpose
You are evaluating applications for the **School of Health Professions Research Seed Grant Program**. Each criterion must be scored **based only on the application’s content**, not on your personal knowledge of the applicant or team.

## Scoring Scale
- **Exemplary (Full Points Range)** – Fully meets and exceeds expectations; compelling evidence for success; highly persuasive.
- **Adequate (Upper-Mid Range)** – Meets most expectations; minor gaps or weaknesses.
- **Minimal Evidence (Mid-Lower Range)** – Major gaps; insufficient supporting evidence.
- **Insufficient Evidence (Lowest Range)** – Does not meet expectations; unclear, unsupported, or irrelevant.

Scores should be given as **whole integers only** (no decimals or fractions). Provide **comments** explaining your reasoning for each score.
